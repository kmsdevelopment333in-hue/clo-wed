The Atelier

Crafted by Hand, Worn by Heart

Every Maison Cloure gown passes through 180 hours of handwork before it ever meets its owner.

Our embroiderers bead each petal by hand in rose-gold thread. Our pleaters fold silk in a single unbroken motion. From the first muslin toile to the final pressed hem, each dress is an heirloom — measured, fitted, and finished to the half-millimetre for one silhouette only: yours.
