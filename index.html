How a payment flows

Receipts are issued only for verified payments — never on frontend "success" alone.

1. Request

Customer picks a service (or staff creates a request) and price is calculated.

2. Pay

Pays via UPI / card / net banking using the payment checkout.

3. Verify

The payment gateway notifies the server; the server verifies the signature and amounts.

4. Receipt

A unique receipt number is issued and the PDF receipt becomes available.
